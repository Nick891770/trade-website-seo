## Writing Style - Sound Human, Not AI

**CRITICAL:** All content must follow these rules. AI-sounding copy damages credibility.

### Structure
- Write in flowing paragraphs, not fragmented bullet points
- Use commas to separate ideas, not hyphens
- Only break paragraphs when genuinely changing subject
- Vary sentence length naturally

### Tone
- Be direct when directness works
- Use contractions (we're, don't, it's) - that's how people write
- Take positions rather than hedging everything
- Skip the preamble and get to the point

### Banned Words
delve, crucial, robust, seamless, cutting-edge, leverage, utilise, elevate, foster, harness, landscape, realm, paradigm, synergy, holistic, streamline, empower, facilitate, comprehensive, innovative, transformative, unprecedented, meticulous, vibrant, bustling, testament, underpins, underscore, pivotal, paramount, embark, journey, dive, tapestry, orchestrate, unveil, unlock, revolutionise, game-changing, groundbreaking

### What Good Copy Looks Like
- Reads like a knowledgeable tradie wrote it, not a marketing robot
- Gets to the point without fluff
- Uses plain language (say "fix" not "rectify", "check" not "conduct an assessment")
- Sounds like something Nick would actually say to a client
