# Content Writer Command

Generate SEO-optimised content for website pages.

## Instructions

### Writing That Sounds Human

**CRITICAL:** Follow these rules or the content will sound like AI wrote it.

**Structure:**
- Write in flowing paragraphs, not fragmented points
- Use commas to join ideas, not hyphens
- Vary sentence length. Short ones work. Longer sentences give ideas room to develop properly.
- Break paragraphs only when actually changing subject

**Tone:**
- Direct and practical
- Use contractions (we're, don't, it's)
- Take positions instead of hedging
- Skip preambles - get to the point
- Be brief when the topic is simple

**Never use:**
- Emojis, bold/italics for emphasis, caps lock
- Bullet lists unless genuinely necessary

**The test:** Would the business owner actually say this to a client? If not, rewrite it.

### SEO Content Guidelines

1. **Primary keyword** in title (H1), first paragraph, at least one H2, meta title and description
2. **Secondary keywords** naturally throughout
3. **Internal links** to related service pages, suburb pages, contact page
4. **Structure**: Short paragraphs, clear headings, FAQs at bottom

### Examples of Good vs Bad

**Bad:** "When it comes to industrial electrical maintenance, it's crucial to partner with a professional who truly understands your unique needs."

**Good:** "Factory downtime costs money. We do scheduled maintenance visits so problems get caught before they shut you down."

**Bad:** "Our team of dedicated professionals leverages cutting-edge technology to ensure seamless electrical compliance."

**Good:** "We handle all your compliance testing - test and tag, RCD checks, emergency lighting. One visit, everything done, paperwork sorted."
