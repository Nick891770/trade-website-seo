# Suburb Page Generator Command

Create SEO-optimised suburb landing pages for service areas.

## Instructions

### Page Structure

```
URL: /electrician-[suburb]/
Title: Electrician [Suburb] | Industrial & Commercial | Laws Electrical
H1: Electrician [Suburb] - Industrial & Commercial Electrical Services

## Local Industrial Electrician in [Suburb]
[2-3 paragraphs about serving this area, mention nearby landmarks/industrial estates]

## Services We Provide in [Suburb]
- Industrial maintenance and repairs
- Test and tag services
- RCD testing
- Emergency lighting testing
- Switchboard upgrades
- Machine wiring and repairs

## Why Choose Laws Electrical in [Suburb]
[Differentiation, local knowledge, response times]

## [Suburb] Service Area
[Mention nearby suburbs also serviced, map if possible]

## Get a Quote for [Suburb]
[Contact form or CTA]

## Frequently Asked Questions
[3-5 local FAQs with schema markup]
```

### Content Guidelines

1. **Unique content** - Don't duplicate across suburbs
2. **Local relevance** - Mention specific industrial estates, major employers, landmarks, travel times
3. **Natural keyword use** - "electrician [suburb]", "[suburb] electrical services"
4. **Internal linking** - Link to service pages and nearby suburb pages
5. **Schema** - Include LocalBusiness with specific areaServed

### Writing Rules - Sound Human

**Do:**
- Write in flowing paragraphs
- Use contractions (we're, don't, it's)
- Vary sentence length naturally
- Be specific about the area
- Sound like a local tradie, not a marketing agency

**Don't:**
- Use bullet lists for everything
- Start with "Welcome to our [suburb] page"
- Use banned words from the style guide
- Write generic content that could apply to any suburb

**Example of what NOT to write:**
"When it comes to finding a reliable electrician in Sumner, it's crucial to choose a professional who understands your unique needs."

**Example of what TO write:**
"We're based just down the road from Sumner and know the industrial area well. Most of our callouts here are to the factories along Archerfield Road. If something goes wrong, we can usually be on site within the hour."

## Usage

Specify suburb name when running:
`/suburb-page Sumner`
